The thesis

Two kinds of answers the fabric never mixes.

Most systems blur a model's guess together with a hard fact and hand you one number you cannot audit. Orca sorts every question by kind and answers each one in the only way that is honest for it.

Learned judgment

Calibrated confidence.

choicejudgescore → calibrated probability

The model rates its own confidence. A calibration layer and an admission policy then decide whether that confidence has earned an automatic answer or needs a human review.

automatic · review · decline
Deterministic authority

Proven or declined.

resolvecomputeverifyconformrequire → evidence-backed verdict

Facts do not get a confidence score. A deterministic engine resolves, computes, verifies, conforms and requires. It returns an evidence-backed verdict rather than a probability, and it never touches the model.

every verdict carries a receipt
The authority layer

Five operations that do not guess.

Each one wraps machinery that already fails closed and names its own gaps. Give it what it needs and it establishes. Leave something out and it declines by name instead of hallucinating.

RESOLVE

Definitional authority

Resolve a term against configured authorities. When two disagree it reports a conflict instead of picking a side.

resolve "UAS" → established · aero
COMPUTE

Deterministic calculators

Route a request to a registered calculator. It never invents a missing input. It asks for it.

15% of 80 → 12 · exact
VERIFY

Candidate against contract

Run a candidate through deterministic checks. It establishes only when every check passes.

valid JSON + keys → established · 2/2
CONFORM

Bind output to shape

Reformat model content to a declared constraint. If reformatting cannot close the gap it declines.

numbered → three dash bullets
REQUIRE

Ground the inputs

Establish only when every required input is present. If an artifact is absent it asks for the artifact instead of fabricating one.

absent JSON payload → decline
+ ROUTING

Learned and calibrated

The learned router rates each request and a calibrated admission policy decides how far it may act on its own.

Built to extend

Bring your own model. Add your own authorities.

Orca is not one calculator. It is two open planes behind one engine. Path loss is a single calculator on the deterministic plane, and percentages arrived the same way. Your own model plugs into the learned plane.

The deterministic plane

Add your own authorities.

Register a COMPUTE calculator. Compose a VERIFY contract from checks. Add a whole new operation the way CONFORM and REQUIRE were added. None of it edits frozen contract code.

registry.register(Calculator( name="arithmetic.percentage", triggers=("percent of", "% of"), parse=parse_percentage, # text to inputs compute=run_percentage, # inputs to value ))
The learned plane

Bring your own model.

Put your own model behind the provider interface, local or frontier. It proposes as a calibrated scorer over your answer space and never speaks as an authority. The deterministic layer grounds and gates what it says before anything is admitted.

engine = DecisionEngine( provider=your_model, # local or frontier authority_provider=authorities, )

Ground it: send your model's answer and your evidence to VERIFY and every claim is checked back against your data, then returned as admit, decline or review with a receipt. No model runs the check.

Where it runs

Small enough to run where your data lives.

// on-prem · offline · single-GPU

Nothing leaves.

At about 300 million parameters, Orca is trained from scratch and runs on your own hardware. Keep it air-gapped if you need to. The evidence your decisions rest on never has to touch a third-party API.

// typed-contract-v1 · byte-frozen

Frozen contracts.

The typed decision contract is frozen byte for byte and guarded in CI. A verdict from today reproduces exactly tomorrow. That is the difference between a demo and something you can build a process on.

How a decision flows

One request. Each question answered in kind.

Parse

One typed request carries many questions and is validated against a frozen contract.

Split

The engine sorts each question into learned or authority and answers it apart from the others.

Decide

Learned questions pass a calibrated admission policy. Authority questions get a deterministic verdict.

Return

Every answer comes back with its evidence and its admission. Authority answers add a receipt.
